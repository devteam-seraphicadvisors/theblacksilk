## 4. Mission Page (`/mission`)

### 4.1 Introduction
```text
The Black Silk exists because digital technology now shapes nearly every part of human life, yet the people most affected by it, everyday users, are often the least equipped to influence how it is built and governed. Our mission is to close that gap: to bring lawmakers, policymakers, academicians, and technology professionals into direct, structured dialogue, so that the rules governing digital technology in India are shaped with the people they affect, not just for them.
```

### 4.2 Our Vision and Values
- **Inclusivity**: Every stakeholder, from regulators to end users, has a seat at the table in shaping the future of digital technology in India.
- **Impartiality**: New opportunities in technology should be available to everyone equally, not concentrated among those with the most resources or influence.
- **Accountability**: Those who build and deploy digital technology bear responsibility for its consequences, and our discussions hold that responsibility in view.
- **Rigor**: Our positions are built on research, consultation, and evidence, not assumption.
- **National Relevance**: We root our work in the specific legal, regulatory, and social realities of India, rather than borrowing frameworks built for other jurisdictions.

### 4.3 How We Work (4 Core Pillars)
*(Layout Note: Designed to be presented in 4 columns or grid cards with a contextual graphic/photo above each item)*

1. **Convene**:
   We create the room where lawmakers, regulators, academicians, and technology professionals can speak frankly about the hardest questions in Indian digital policy.
2. **Consult**:
   We open our discussions to public consultation, so that solutions are shaped by the people they affect, not decided without them.
3. **Research and Publish**:
   We turn discussion into documented guidance, giving our conclusions a lasting, citable form through articles and research papers.
4. **Advocate**:
   We carry those conclusions to the policymakers and institutions positioned to act on them, so that dialogue translates into real thresholds and standards within India.

## 5. Approach Page (`/approach`)

### 5.1 Introduction
```text
The Black Silk's work rests on a simple idea: the best answers to digital technology's hardest questions come from structured, inclusive dialogue among the people who understand the problem from different sides, not from any single voice working alone. This page sets out the principles that guide that dialogue and the process we follow to turn it into practical outcomes.
```

### 5.2 Core Principles
*(Layout Note: Designed to be laid out in 4 columns/cards with an accompanying icon or illustration)*

- **Stakeholder-Led**: Every discussion includes the people actually affected by the issue at hand, not only legal or policy experts.
- **Evidence-Based**: Our conclusions are grounded in research and real-world input, not assumption or precedent borrowed from elsewhere.
- **Transparent Process**: How we reach a position is as important as the position itself, and our process is open to scrutiny.
- **Non-Partisan**: We do not represent any single industry, government body, or interest group; our role is to convene, not to advocate for one side.

### 5.3 Our Methodology: The Black Silk Framework
Every discussion convened follows this uniform four-stage process:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE BLACK SILK FRAMEWORK                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   │
   ├─► 1. IDENTIFY THE ISSUE
   │   • Monitor emerging developments in digital tech & legal/ethical questions
   │   • Flag issues with direct relevance to India's regulatory context
   │   • Consult early with members and partners to confirm merit
   │   • Define specific questions the discussion needs to answer
   │
   ├─► 2. CONVENE STAKEHOLDERS
   │   • Identify and invite diverse voices (lawmakers, academicians, users)
   │   • Structure format: public consultation, closed roundtable, committee review
   │   • Share comprehensive background material in advance
   │   • Set out clear scope and intended outcomes before beginning
   │
   ├─► 3. DELIBERATE AND ANALYZE
   │   • Facilitate open, frank discussion without favoring any interest
   │   • Capture full range of positions raised, including dissent
   │   • Stress-test proposed solutions against practical & ethical tests
   │   • Map where consensus exists and where it does not
   │
   └─► 4. PUBLISH AND ADVOCATE
       • Document conclusions in clear, citable formats
       • Publish findings as articles, whitepapers, or position statements
       • Share outcomes directly with policymakers and institutions
       • Track whether and how resulting recommendations are adopted
```

## 7. Membership Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Is membership annual?
> **Answer**: Yes. All memberships run for 12 months from the date of payment and must be renewed annually to remain active.

### Q2. When does my membership start, and how do I renew?
> **Answer**: Your membership activates immediately upon payment. You'll receive a renewal reminder before expiry; renewing before the Flagship Summit each year also qualifies you for member-rate summit registration.

### Q3. Can I switch tiers mid-year, e.g. if I get promoted or cross the 5-year mark?
> **Answer**: Yes. You can upgrade at any time by paying the difference between tiers. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal date.

### Q4. What happens if our team grows past 10 or 20 members mid-year?
> **Answer**: You can continue on your current tier's pricing for the rest of that membership year. At your next renewal, you'll move to the tier appropriate for your team size at that time.

### Q5. Is there a refund if I want to cancel?
> **Answer**: Memberships are non-refundable once payment is processed, since benefits activate immediately.

### Q6. Who is eligible for the Advocate tiers, and what about non-lawyers?
> **Answer**: Any advocate enrolled with a State Bar Council in India, split by years since enrollment (under 5 yrs / 5+ yrs). The Non-lawyer professional tier is open to anyone in policy, tech, or academia working on tech law and digital policy issues.

### Q7. How does Company or Law Firm membership work?
> **Answer**: You list all covered members by name and email at signup. Each named member gets individual login access and benefits at the specified tier (In-house Counsel for Company, Advocate 5+ yrs for Law Firm). One-time substitutions are allowed if someone leaves during the year.

### Q8. Does membership guarantee a speaking slot at the Flagship Summit?
> **Answer**: Only the Advocate (5+ yrs) individual tier and Law Firm (11-20 members) group tier include a speaking or panel opportunity, subject to programming decisions. Other tiers get discounted or priority registration, not a guaranteed slot.
